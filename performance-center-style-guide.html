Type

Bornia only: Medium, Bold, and Black. There is no Regular. Body copy is Medium; emphasis inside a sentence uses italic or Bold, never a fourth weight. Headings are Black and uppercase. Section subheads are Bold and sentence case.

Interactions

Class cards

Desktop: hover flips. Touch: tap flips; a drag greater than 8px does not flip. Only one card is flipped at a time. The Book button is a real link and does not toggle the card. Flip runs 0.7s on cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1).

Marquees

Locally-Owned masonry and the Next Level mobile rail loop at the same visual speed: 70s for the masonry track, with Next Level duration scaled from that track’s length. Pause is not required. Gap between images stays 12px while moving. Reduced-motion users get no transform animation on flips.

Quote slider

Desktop and tablet: 2×2 static grid, no arrows. ≤767: one quote at a time, previous/next, swipe, and dots. Controls are white on the teal section (not teal-on-teal).

Voice & content

Typesetting follows these copy rules. If a line fights the layout, change the layout, not the voice.

Do

  • Sentence-case subheads and class names: “Personal training”, not “PERSONAL TRAINING”.
  • Uppercase display headings, and let them wrap.
  • Keep “Purple Patch” Medium in running copy. Bold italic only on the dictionary term.
  • HYROX is the only class subhead, and the only one in teal.
  • Quotes stay anonymous. No member names or cities.
  • Prefer em dashes and concrete coaching language.

Don’t

  • Don’t shrink a heading to force one line.
  • Don’t set body in muted gray or a light gray.
  • Don’t write “mere mortals”, “every human”, or cute gym clichés.
  • Don’t add a byline, star rating, or logo row on quotes.
  • Don’t invent a third CTA label style.

Do / don’t

Do

  • Use Bornia Medium / Bold / Black only.
  • Keep body at 20px ink.
  • Pair solid teal with outline teal — nothing else.
  • Match section padding top and bottom.
  • Let display headings wrap on small screens.
  • Keep quotes anonymous.
  • Follow the page order and surface for each section.
  • Keep booking as a right-hand drawer, not a modal.

Don’t

  • Don’t use light-gray body copy.
  • Don’t add a sticky nav or sticky bottom CTA.
  • Don’t put names on testimonial cards.
  • Don’t stretch the logo — always width: auto.
  • Don’t introduce a third button style or gold CTAs.
  • Don’t add drop shadows to cards.
  • Don’t put decorative icons in content.
  • Don’t link this style guide from the Center page.
