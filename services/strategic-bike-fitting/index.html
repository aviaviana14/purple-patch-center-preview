The Strategic Fitting Studio at the Purple Patch Center

Our purpose-built studio provides the backdrop that allows Chris Soden to go beyond traditional bike fit protocols and offer a tailored bike-fitting experience designed around you.

The studio includes on-site access to high quality motion capture, IMU-based motion data analysis, PNOE’s metabolic analysis device, and personalized expertise to arrive at your optimal riding position.

You can also include custom insole solutions for foot comfort, as well as an array for equipment and saddle testing to ensure a perfect fit and comfort for your situation.

Meet Chris Soden

Chris regularly works with recreational cyclists and triathletes of all levels, as well as WorldTour professionals and Olympians.

With more than 30 years of experience in the cycling and bike fitting industries, Chris brings a wealth of information, professional guidance, and veteran perspectives on the evolution of the sport to every fit he does. Chris is guided by the concept of "precision and purpose" during each fit, recognizing the importance of treating each rider as a true individual and ensuring they leave the fit excited to head out on their next ride.
